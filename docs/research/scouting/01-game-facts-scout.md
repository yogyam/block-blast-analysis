## 2. Engine and tech stack

- **Engine: Cocos (Cocos Creator with JSB), not Unity** [R→C: two independent RE sources, no official statement]. Ayoships: "native Cocos library", gameplay JS at `main/index.45fec.js`, rule "traits" encrypted with **XXTEA + Brotli**, tools jadx + Ghidra — https://ayoships.com/breakdowns/block-blast. unsupo repo: `libtask.so` (6.7 MB, unstripped C++) holding `AlgorithmCore`, `BinaryBoard`, `AlgoSelect` — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/unsupo/Block-Blast-Solver/main/README.md. AppGoblin's "Unity" entry is Unity Ads, not the engine.
- **Architecture.** Thin store binary; ~700 server-configured "traits" (A/B arms), 254 selected per install, 101 hot-downloaded post-install as encrypted JS from `bband1.afafb.com` / `bbios1.afafb.com` [R] — ayoships; unsupo independently names `afafb.com`.
- **SDKs (AppGoblin scan, Sept 2026).** 18 ad networks incl. Google/AdMob, Meta, **AppLovin (MAX mediation)**, Unity, **ByteDance/Pangle**; AppsFlyer; Firebase; ThinkingData; Volcengine; Bugsnag; iOS also Sentry, Lottie, SDWebImage [R] — https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.block.juggle, https://www.appgoblin.info/apps/1617391485. DT Exchange added for all placements (+8% ARPDAU) [C]. Ayoships saw MAX eCPM callbacks (`callNativeGetMaxEcpmNumber`) feeding game rules [R]. A Proton study lists Block Blast among games sending data to Russia/China-based firms [R] — https://www.techradar.com/vpn/vpn-privacy-security/your-favorite-free-mobile-game-could-be-sharing-your-data-with-russia-and-china-proton-warns
- **On-device ML (ayoships only) [R].** XGBoost eCPM regressor (`xgboost_regression_model111.json`, 59 trees, 27 inputs → `finalEcpm`); XGBoost level-difficulty classifier (500 trees, 52 inputs, `predict_travel_difficulty_dead`); LightGBM next-day-activity model (1,000 trees, 93 inputs). Server-side: churn, LTV, revive-click, xDeepFM.

## 3. Rules

- **Core [C].** 8×8, 3 pieces per tray, refill only after all three placed, no rotation, rows+columns clear, no gravity, game over when nothing fits — App Store listing; unsupo GAME_MECHANICS https://raw.githubusercontent.com/unsupo/Block-Blast-Solver/main/docs/GAME_MECHANICS.md
- **Piece set [R, single source].** unsupo claims `AlgorithmCore::g_arrShape` = **43 entries** (two duplicate pairs → ~41 unique): 1×1; bars 2–5 both orientations; 2×2, 3×3, 2×3/3×2; L/J tetrominoes (8), S/Z (4), T (4), 3-cell corners (4), 3×3 "big L" (4), and 2- and 3-cell **diagonals** — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/unsupo/Block-Blast-Solver/main/docs/SHAPES_CATALOG.md. Fan sites say 19 or 34 [S].
- **Scoring — contested.** unsupo: 1 pt/cell + `10 × lines² × combo`, combo = consecutive clearing moves, resets on a non-clear [R]. Fan wiki: 10/line + flat streak bonus (+10, +20…) [S] — https://blockblast-free-nocms-publish.pages.dev/wiki/scoring-and-combos. No official doc.
- **Modes.** Classic endless; **Adventure** ("Chapter"/"travel" in code) with jigsaw/pixel-art levels; Daily Puzzles [C]. Monetization: bottom banner, interstitial after every game-over, one rewarded revive per run [R] — https://www.gamigion.com/?p=16408, Applixir. Ayoships adds conditional extra revives (rewarded eCPM ≥ 2× interstitial), a free ad-less "pink" revive when churn-risk ≥ 0.69, and early revive offers for players who accept >75% of revives [R].

## 4. The "rigged" discourse

**Player hypotheses (pre-decompile, 2024–25):** (a) pieces chosen from board state, not random; (b) a wall around ~1,500 / just past your high score; (c) easy pieces after a losing streak ("god mode" — Khripin); (d) suspiciously generous openings; (e) Android worse than iOS; (f) older builds (7.6.3) fairer; (g) same piece repeated. Sources: https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1617391485/block-blast/reviews ("not random, but chose by how your board looks"), https://glotier.com/games/block-blast (40% of sampled negative reviews allege rigging; 73% excessive ads), TikTok Nov 2024 https://www.tiktok.com/@blockblast.yap5/video/7443009472160746784, Feb 2025 https://www.tiktok.com/@r3atomiz3r/video/7471357292986780959, skeptical YouTube reviews (~Jan 2025) saying "no concrete evidence" [S]. Solver-site FAQs are contradictory SEO filler [S].

**Developer statements.** No statement on randomness exists. But Hungry Studio's own Stevie Awards essay says "A/B tests help customize difficulty levels, allowing players to progress at a pace that suits their skill level" and that **Block AI Robot** analyzes "player behavior in real-time to create an adaptive experience" [C] — https://blog.stevieawards.com/hubfs/apsa25%20Gold%20entries%20PDFs/Judge-20977-Hungry%20Studio%20-%20Block%20Blast-%20for%20Best%20Mobile%20Apps-Casual%20Games.pdf. Jan 2026 PR: 10,000+ A/B tests in 2025, 300+ parallel/day, Block AI simulation [C]. Jan 2025 interview: 50 tests/week, 97% fail, growth "heavily relied on UA" [C] — https://mobilegamer.biz/block-blast-maker-hungry-studio-a-b-tested-its-way-to-the-top-of-the-charts/. **Hungry Studio has not responded** to the October allegations (mobilegamer, The Star, Gamigion all got no reply) [C].

## 5. Prior reverse-engineering

- **Ayo Omolewa / ayoships (Oct 15 2026) — the primary source** [R; evidence bundles published, decryption key withheld, no independent replication]. Android 10.8.2 / iOS 7.4.9. Claims: "lethal puzzle" generator **algorithm 24001** (`ZHI_SI_TI`=9, 致死题, native `func_death_blcok`) keeps a hand only if *no* placement order survives (171 placements tested on a sample board); `RewardHighValueAdHarvestTrait` (last 3 rewarded ads completed, loaded ad ≥ **3.5×** last eCPM, score ≥ high score + **1,000**); `PersonalMarginBreakTrait` (20% chance per deal once 10% past record); `MaturityDevelopmentV2Trait` (habit+skill+ad-history score vs 65 threshold → easy fills); `FailStreakStrategyTrait` (easier pieces after 2 sub-record games); `FastHandPunishTrait` (<4.2 s/set avg and >93% of record); `AdPlusMinusStrategyTrait` (+up to 10% of high score after revive); `MABXiBaiTrait` (6-arm bandit on lethal-puzzle timing: never/4/6/10/~17/30 min); `MultiClearBoostThenKillTrait` (怼死不敏感 server labels). Country/device drives predicted eCPM (US iPhone 25.48 vs Brazil 8.08 vs India 5.59). Coverage: https://www.gamigion.com/block-blasts-1m-day-ad-logic-just-got-cracked/, https://mobilegamer.biz/this-creator-says-he-decompiled-block-blast-candy-crush-and-subway-surfers-to-reveal-their-monetisation-secrets/, https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/10/28/is-block-blast-rigged-a-developer-decompiled-the-viral-game-to-find-out, https://pressforplay.com/blog/block-blast-ad-logic-teardown/, HN 122 pts, no verification in thread https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50217946
- **unsupo/Block-Blast-Solver (created Oct 23 2026, 0 stars)** [R/S]. Claims Unicorn-emulated extraction of 43 shapes at `0x507904`; symbols `SafetyCheckLogicComponent::hasLiveWay` (solvability DFS, 8 retries then safe pool), `handleWeightControl`, source names `AlgoSelectBlockDealDeathState.cpp`, `AlgoSelectBlockRandomNoDie.cpp`, `AlgoSelectBlockAvoidClearBoard.cpp`, `AlgoSelectBlockEntropy3MediumIOS.cpp`, `selectBlockStopRefreshScore` ("终止刷分"); pools SAFE/HARD/STANDARD, `coverRate` thresholds 0.58/0.28, 17 score tiers to 8,400 with hard-piece odds 5→40%. **Caveats:** internal inconsistencies (52 pool shapes vs 43; three library names), no artifacts, reads partly LLM-generated; but independently corroborates `afafb.com`, `RewardHighValueAdHarvestTrait`, `AdPlusMinusStrategyTrait` — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/unsupo/Block-Blast-Solver/main/docs/REVERSE_ENGINEERING_REPORT.md, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/unsupo/Block-Blast-Solver/main/docs/DEEP_REVERSE_ENGINEERING_SECRETS.md

## What we still don't know

1. No independent replication of ayoships; the XXTEA key and bypass steps are unpublished. Whether unsupo is genuinely independent is unclear.
2. The baseline (no-trait) generator: RNG, weights, whether `hasLiveWay` always runs.
3. Exact scoring formula.
4. Prevalence: which of ~700 arms a typical player gets; whether the Apple Arcade build carries traits.
5. Hungry Studio's position on randomness — silence so far.
6. Reddit sentiment (blocked) and the "7.6.3 was fairer" claim.
7. Conflicting launch dates, 2024 rank, Cocos version, Tencent deal status, cause of the Play Store delisting.
